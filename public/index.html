THE METHOD

Seven checks between a question and an answer.

  1. 1Resolve

    Match the typed address to Wake County’s canonical address point and parcel.

  2. 2Retrieve

    Query each public source by parcel, address, or a clearly labeled radius.

  3. 3Classify

    Keep complaints, permits, reported incidents, property facts, and hazards distinct.

  4. 4Explain

    Attach the official source and the records-request path where open data stops.

  5. 5Summarize

    Use AI only after retrieval, bounded to the records returned by the sources.

  6. 6Cite

    Show match method, scope, retrieval time, and evidence IDs.

  7. 7Abstain

    Say what remains unknown when the address match or source coverage is incomplete.
